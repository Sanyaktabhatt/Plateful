**Chocolate Chip Cookies**

- 2 c. sugar
- 1/4 c. butter
- 1 c. flour
- 2 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 c. chocolate chips

1. In bowl, beat together sugar, butter, flour, salt and vanilla.
2. Add eggs.
3. Fold in chocolate chips.
4. Put in greased 9 x 13-inch pan.
